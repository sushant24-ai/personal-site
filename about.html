Hello, my name is Sushant. I am a Senior/Staff Platform & DevSecOps Engineer with deep expertise in building and scaling AI Platforms. My work sits at the intersection of cloud infrastructure, security, and applied artificial intelligence.

I specialize in designing production-grade AI infrastructure that enables organizations to deploy, monitor, and secure their AI/ML workloads at scale. From Kubernetes clusters optimized for GPU workloads to implementing robust DevSecOps pipelines for AI applications, I focus on making advanced AI accessible and operationally sound.

My passion lies in bridging the gap between cutting-edge AI research and real-world production systems. I believe that the most impactful AI systems are those that are not only technically sophisticated but also secure, observable, and maintainable.

On this blog, I share practical insights from building AI platforms, lessons learned from production deployments, and deep dives into the technologies shaping the future of AI infrastructure.
